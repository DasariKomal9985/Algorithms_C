Improves insertion sort by allowing big jumps (gaps). As gap reduces to 1 → becomes normal insertion sort.

How it works (logic)

Choose a gap (e.g., n/2).

Perform gapped insertion sort.

Reduce gap (e.g., gap = gap/2).

Finish with gap = 1.

Complexity

Time: depends on gap sequence

Worst: O(n²)

Best: ~O(n^(3/2)) or sometimes O(n log² n)

Use when: Faster than basic sorts for medium-sized arrays.
